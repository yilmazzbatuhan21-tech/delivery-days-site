Delivery Days (English)

Start with a tiny garage on a canyon road and grow the biggest delivery company in town.

Sign restaurants

Wrap shops, pide bakeries, burger joints and cafés, each with its own owner and orders.

Grow your fleet

Expand the garage, add scooters and make your riders faster.

Train your staff

As your staff level up, registers fill on their own while you focus on growth.

Earn while away

Your riders keep working the shift even when the game is closed.

Chapters: First Neighborhood, Campus and Coast Road. A fourth chapter, the Old Bazaar, is coming soon.

  • Free to play on iPhone, with optional in-app purchases and opt-in rewarded ads.
  • No account or login. Your progress is stored on your device.
  • Available in English and Turkish. The game follows your iPhone's language, and you can switch it in Settings.
