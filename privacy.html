Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026 · Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what data the iPhone game Delivery Days ("the game") processes, why, and what choices you have.

In short

  • There is no account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or precise location.
  • Your game save stays on your phone and is not sent to us.
  • If your version of the game has analytics, anonymous in-game events (for example which chapter you finished) go to our own analytics service under a random ID. This data is not linked to you, we do not store your IP address, and we never use it for ads or tracking. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob. Your advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt.
  • Apple takes the payment. We never see your card details.
  • If you live in the United States, see Your US state privacy rights below.
  • You can always reach us at bygamess21@gmail.com.

1. Who is responsible

The developer of the game is the controller of the data described here. The developer's name is shown as the seller on the App Store page. For questions and requests: bygamess21@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your phone

To run the game, the following is stored only on your phone and is not sent to us:

  • Game save: your money, gems, restaurants and their levels, goals, chapter progress, the permanent items you bought (for example No Ads) and the time of the last save.
  • Settings: your sound, music, vibration, day/night and language choices, whether the notification permission was asked, and the scheduled notification times.

Deleting the game deletes this data. iOS may include app data in your phone's own backup (iCloud or a computer). That backup is controlled by Apple and you; we cannot access it.

The game does not use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, precise location or health data. There is no place in the game where you type text or share content with others.

3. Analytics (our own service)

To improve the game we use our own analytics service, DD Analitik. It is first-party: we built it and run it ourselves, and the data does not go to an analytics company. It helps us see, for example, which tutorial step is hard, how long chapters take and which errors happen.

Does your version send analytics? Versions of the game that send analytics have an analytics switch in the game's Settings. If there is no such switch in your Settings, your version sends no analytics data.

When analytics is on, the game sends:

  • In-game events: tutorial steps, chapter and goal progress, restaurant deals and levels, garage and scooter levels, gems and in-game money earned and spent (rounded), staff cards, the daily login calendar, pages opened, game starts and session lengths, how long you were away before coming back, your answers to the notification and tracking permission prompts, and when the rating prompt was shown.
  • Ad events: which rewarded ad was opened, watched to the end, closed early or failed to load.
  • Purchase events: product, price, currency and whether the purchase went through. No payment details.
  • Error information: the type of errors the game recovered from and where in the code they happened. File paths, numbers and addresses are removed on your phone before sending.
  • Device and version information: game version, iOS version, device generation (for example "iPhone 15 family", not the exact model), memory range and device language.
  • A random analytics ID: the game creates a random ID for itself the first time analytics runs. It only groups events from the same installation and contains nothing about you. Versions with analytics show it in Settings, under the analytics switch.

What is not sent: your name, email, phone number, precise location, advertising identifier (IDFA) and Apple's identifier for vendors (IDFV) are never sent to this service. The analytics ID is not linked to your identity or your Apple Account, and it is not combined with ad network data or any other data. We do not use this data to identify you, contact you, show you ads or track you, and we do not sell it.

IP address and country: when the game sends events, your phone's IP address reaches our infrastructure provider Cloudflare, as with any internet connection. We do not record your IP address. We keep only the country code (for example "US" or "TR") that Cloudflare derives from the request.

Where and for how long: the data is processed on our behalf on Cloudflare's infrastructure (Workers and the D1 database), with the database set up in Western Europe. Raw event batches are deleted 3 days after processing. Records tied to an installation (first launch day, days played, progress, purchase and error records) are deleted after 400 days (about 13 months) at the latest. After that, only daily totals without any ID remain, for example "how often the shop was opened that day".

Turning it off: switch off analytics in the game's Settings and the game collects and sends no analytics data. Events waiting on your phone and the analytics ID are deleted. If you switch it back on, a new ID with no link to the old one is created. Deleting the game also deletes the analytics ID.

4. Ads: Google AdMob

The game has rewarded ads only. An ad opens only when you tap for a reward: the "x2 income" button, offers from the character Deal Ferdi, or doubling your daily gems or the money earned while you were away. There are no banner ads and no full-screen ads that open by themselves.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google's software processes the following to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud: IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, device identifiers, the ads you see and tap, and crash and performance information.

Tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency)

iOS asks you once whether the game may track you across other companies' apps and websites for advertising.

  • If you allow it: Google may use your advertising identifier (IDFA) to show more relevant ads and to measure them.
  • If you don't: the IDFA is not shared. You can still watch rewarded ads, but they are less personalized. The rest of the game works the same.
  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking on your iPhone.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, personalized ads are shown only with your consent, which is asked through Google's consent message.

If you live in a US state covered by Google's US state privacy message, the game shows that message with a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" option. See section 10.

If you buy "No Ads" or "No Ads + Daily Gems", no ads are shown and rewards are given directly.

How Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Purchases

  • Purchases are made through Apple's App Store. Your payment and billing details stay with Apple; we cannot see them.
  • The game only learns from Apple which product was bought and that the payment went through. The item is written to the game save on your phone.
  • Permanent items (No Ads, No Ads + Daily Gems, Permanent 2x Income, Starter Pack) can be restored with "Restore purchases" in the game's Settings, using the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

6. Notifications

The game uses local notifications only (for example "Your register is full!"). They are scheduled on your phone; we have no notification server. Permission is asked once, after the first chapter goal. You can turn them off at any time in Settings › Notifications › Delivery Days.

7. Legal basis and transfers

The game is offered on the App Store in most countries and regions (not in mainland China or Vietnam). We process data under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and, where they apply, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR:

  • Running the game and delivering what you bought: performance of our contract with you.
  • Analytics and error tracking (if your version has analytics): our legitimate interest in improving the game; your consent where the law requires it. You can object at any time by switching analytics off in Settings.
  • Tracking and personalized ads: your consent (the iOS tracking prompt and, where required, Google's consent message).

Our analytics data is stored in Cloudflare's database in Western Europe; requests pass through the Cloudflare server nearest to you. Google's servers may be located outside Türkiye (for example in the European Union and the United States). These transfers rely on the safeguards these providers offer (such as their data processing terms and standard contractual clauses) and, where required, your consent.

If you live in the United States, see Your US state privacy rights.

8. Children

The game is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are worried about your child's data in this game, write to us and we will act on it.

We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

9. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK and, where it applies, the GDPR, you have the right to ask about, access, correct or delete your data, to object to its processing, and to withdraw consent. You can also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or the data protection authority of your country.

  • Data on your phone: delete the game.
  • Tracking: turn it off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Analytics data: if your version has analytics, switch it off at any time in the game's Settings. To see or delete data already sent, write to bygamess21@gmail.com with the analytics ID shown in Settings. Because there is no account, we cannot find your data any other way.
  • Ad data: for data held by Google, use Google's own tools or write to us. Because there is no account, we may ask for details such as your device model and the dates you played.

We answer requests within 30 days at the latest.

10. Your US state privacy rights

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Utah or Virginia), you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we process about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct or delete it;
  • opt out of targeted advertising and of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information;
  • not be treated differently for using these rights.

We do not sell personal information for money. Rewarded ads in the game are served by Google AdMob, and showing personalized ads may count as "sharing" personal information or "targeted advertising" under some state laws. The information involved is: device identifiers (the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking), approximate location derived from your IP address, the ads you see and tap, and how you use the game (see section 4). Our own analytics data (section 3) is not sold, shared or used for ads. We do not collect sensitive personal information.

How to opt out or make a request

  • In the game: if you are in a state covered by Google's US state privacy message, choose "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" in that message. When that message applies to you, a "Privacy choices" row in the game's Settings lets you change your choice later.
  • On your iPhone: turn off tracking in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • By email: write to bygamess21@gmail.com.

You can also make a request through an authorized agent. Because there is no account, we may ask for details such as your device model, the dates you played or your analytics ID so we can find your data. We answer requests within 30 days at the latest. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer; we will respond to your appeal within 45 days.

11. How we respond to Do Not Track signals

Some browsers and devices can send a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. Because there is no common standard for how to respond to it, the game and this website do not respond to Do Not Track signals. You can still limit ad tracking with the iOS tracking setting and the choices described in Your US state privacy rights.

12. Third parties that collect data over time

Google (AdMob) may collect information about your use of the game over time and across other apps and websites in order to show and measure ads, as described in section 4. Other than Google, we do not allow third parties to collect personal information about your activity over time and across other apps or websites through the game or this website.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or ads. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security.

13. Security and retention

Data the game sends travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data on your phone stays until you delete the game. Our analytics data is kept for the periods in section 3 (about 13 months at the latest), and our analytics dashboard can only be opened with our administrator key. Ad data is kept for Google's retention periods.

14. Changes

If we add a new service or change how we use data, we will update this page and its dates. We will also announce important changes in the game.
