Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what data the iPhone game Delivery Days ("the game") processes, why, and what choices you have.

In short

  • There is no account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or precise location.
  • Your game save stays on your phone and is not sent to us.
  • To improve the game, in-game events (for example which chapter you finished) are sent to GameAnalytics.
  • Rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob. Your advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt.
  • Apple takes the payment. We never see your card details.
  • You can always reach us at (support address coming soon).

1. Who is responsible

The developer of the game is the controller of the data described here. The developer's name is shown as the seller on the App Store page. For questions and requests: (support address coming soon).

2. Data that stays on your phone

To run the game, the following is stored only on your phone and is not sent to us:

  • Game save: your money, gems, restaurants and their levels, goals, chapter progress, the permanent items you bought (for example No Ads) and the time of the last save.
  • Settings: sound and day/night choices, whether the notification permission was asked, and the scheduled notification times.

Deleting the game deletes this data. iOS may include app data in your phone's own backup (iCloud or a computer). That backup is controlled by Apple and you; we cannot access it.

The game does not use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, precise location or health data. There is no place in the game where you type text or share content with others.

3. Analytics: GameAnalytics

We use GameAnalytics to improve the game, for example to see which tutorial step is hard, how long chapters take, and which errors happen. The game sends:

  • In-game events: tutorial steps, chapter and goal progress, restaurant deals and levels, garage and scooter levels, gem and in-game money earned and spent (rounded), pages opened, session lengths, the answer to the notification permission, and the number of errors the game recovered from.
  • Ad events: which rewarded ad was opened, watched to the end, closed early or failed to load.
  • Purchase events: product, price and currency. No payment details.

In addition, the GameAnalytics software processes the device model, iOS version, game version, connection type and session details; a device-level identifier (a random ID or Apple's identifier for vendors, IDFV); and your IP address, from which an approximate location (country, region, city) is derived. The advertising identifier (IDFA) can only be read if you allowed tracking.

We do not use this data to identify you, contact you or show you ads, and we do not sell it. GameAnalytics processes it on our behalf. Details: GameAnalytics privacy notice and retention and deletion policy.

4. Ads: Google AdMob

The game has rewarded ads only. An ad opens only when you tap for a reward: the "Gelir x2" (income x2) button, offers from the character Fırsat Ferdi, or doubling your daily gems or the money earned while you were away. There are no banner ads and no full-screen ads that open by themselves.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google's software processes the following to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud: IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, device identifiers, the ads you see and tap, and crash and performance information.

Tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency)

iOS asks you once whether the game may track you across other companies' apps and websites for advertising.

  • If you allow it: Google may use your advertising identifier (IDFA) to show more relevant ads and to measure them.
  • If you don't: the IDFA is not shared. You can still watch rewarded ads, but they are less personalised. The rest of the game works the same.
  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking on your iPhone.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, your consent for personalised ads is also asked through Google's consent message.

If you buy "Reklamsız" (No Ads) or "Reklamsız + Günlük Elmas" (No Ads + Daily Gems), no ads are shown and rewards are given directly.

How Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Purchases

  • Purchases are made through Apple's App Store. Your payment and billing details stay with Apple; we cannot see them.
  • The game only learns from Apple which product was bought and that the payment went through. The item is written to the game save on your phone.
  • Permanent items (No Ads, No Ads + Daily Gems, Permanent 2x Income, Starter Pack) can be restored from the game's settings with the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

6. Notifications

The game uses local notifications only (for example "your till is full"). They are scheduled on your phone; we have no notification server. Permission is asked once, after the first chapter goal. You can turn them off at any time in Settings › Notifications › Delivery Days.

7. Legal basis and transfers

We process data under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Running the game and delivering what you bought: performance of our contract with you.
  • Analytics and error tracking: our legitimate interest in improving the game; your consent where the law requires it.
  • Tracking and personalised ads: your consent (the iOS tracking prompt and, where required, the consent message).

The servers of GameAnalytics and Google may be located outside Türkiye (for example in the European Union and the United States). These transfers rely on the safeguards these providers offer (such as standard contractual clauses) and, where required, your consent.

8. Children

The game is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are worried about your child's data in this game, write to us and we will act on it.

9. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK and, where it applies, the GDPR, you have the right to ask about, access, correct or delete your data, to object to its processing, and to withdraw consent. You can also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or the data protection authority of your country.

  • Data on your phone: delete the game.
  • Tracking: turn it off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Analytics and ad data: write to (support address coming soon). Because there is no account, we cannot identify you directly; to handle your request we may ask for details such as your device model and the dates you played. For data held by Google you can also use Google's own tools.

We answer requests within 30 days at the latest.

10. Security and retention

Data the game sends travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data on your phone stays until you delete the game. Analytics and ad data are kept for the retention periods of the respective provider.

11. Changes

If we add a new service or change how we use data, we will update this page and its effective date. We will also announce important changes in the game.
