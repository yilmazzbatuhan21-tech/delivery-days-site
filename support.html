Support

Something not working, or have an idea? Check the questions below first; if you can't find an answer, write to us.

Contact us

Email: bygamess21@gmail.com

When reporting a bug, include your iPhone model, iOS version and the game version so we can help faster. The game version is shown at the bottom of the in-game Settings window (tap the portrait button at the top left), for example "Delivery Days · version 1.0.0 (1)".

The game is available in English and Turkish. Button names below are the ones in the English version of the game.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

There is no account or login. Your progress is stored only on your phone and is saved whenever the game closes. We do not keep a copy on our servers.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game or change phones?

Deleting the game deletes the save on your phone, and we cannot bring it back. If you move to a new phone from an iCloud backup or with a direct transfer from your old phone, your game save usually comes along. The game has no separate cloud save.

Permanent items you bought are never lost; you can restore them as described below.

How do I restore my purchases?
  1. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy.
  2. In the game, tap the portrait button at the top left to open Settings.
  3. Under Purchases, tap Restore purchases. The same button is also at the bottom of the Shop.

Restored: No Ads, No Ads + Daily Gems, Permanent 2x Income and the Permanent 2x Income in the Starter Pack. Gem packs are used up when spent, so they are not restored.

I paid but didn't get my item.

Close the game completely and open it again; Apple's confirmation can take a few minutes. For a permanent item, try Restore purchases in Settings. If it still hasn't arrived, email bygamess21@gmail.com with the purchase date and the item name.

Can I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple, so Apple handles refunds. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

When do ads appear? Can I turn them off?

The game only has rewarded ads, and they open only when you tap for a reward: the "x2 income" button, offers from Deal Ferdi, or doubling your daily gems or the money earned while you were away. Watch to the end to get the reward; closing early gives no reward.

With No Ads or No Ads + Daily Gems you get the rewards directly, without ads. Daily limits still apply.

What happens if I tap "Ask App Not to Track"?

Your advertising identifier is not shared. The game and the rewarded ads work the same; the ads are just less personalized. You can change your choice in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking on your iPhone. See the privacy policy for details, and Your US state privacy rights if you live in the United States.

How do I turn off notifications?

On your iPhone, go to Settings › Notifications › Delivery Days. The game does not send notifications between 10 p.m. and 9 a.m.

Do I earn while the game is closed?

Yes. When you come back, the Shift Report shows what you earned while you were away. How long that covers depends on the shifts you have unlocked in the Garage.

Can I reset my progress?

Yes. In Settings, under Progress, tap Reset progress and confirm. Everything you bought with real money (No Ads, No Ads + Daily Gems, Permanent 2x Income and bought gems you haven't spent) is kept.

Which languages is the game in?

English and Turkish. The game opens in Turkish if your iPhone is set to Turkish, and in English otherwise. You can switch at any time with Dil / Language in Settings; the game reopens in the language you pick.

When are new chapters coming?

We are working on the fourth chapter, the Old Bazaar. Updates arrive through the App Store.
